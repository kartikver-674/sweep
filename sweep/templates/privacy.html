Your data

What Sweep keeps, and for how long.

As little as it can, for as short a time as it can. There's no account, no ads and no tracking script. Here is what happens to each thing you give it.

Your résumé

  • The PDF is read and deleted in the same request. It is never saved.
  • The text read from it stays in the app's memory, tied to your browser's session. It's dropped two hours after your last activity, or sooner if the app restarts.
  • To work out your skills and roles, the text goes to Sweep's own language model, which runs on Modal. It answers and keeps nothing. Its log records the size of the request, not the words.
  • The file and its full text never go to the search server, to Apify or to any job site.

Your Apify key (Deep Sweep only)

  • Sweep checks the key with Apify to read your remaining credit, then hands it to the search server. That server holds it in memory until your sweep starts, for 45 minutes at most, and passes it straight to the sweep.
  • The key is never written to disk, a log, a web address or a page. Sweep keeps only a one-way fingerprint of the account, to tell two accounts apart, and the credit figure it shows you.
  • Deep Sweep runs on your Apify account, so Apify sees those searches and bills them to your credit.

Your search and its results

  • When you start a sweep, the search server receives what Sweep worked out from your résumé (roles, skills and their weights, a short summary of your experience) and the preferences you confirmed. Never the file or its full text.
  • The search and its results stay on that server for 48 hours, so you can come back to them, and are then deleted.
  • Searches reach career pages and job boards as ordinary searches: job titles, skills and places, never your name or contact details.

Who else is involved

  • Render hosts this site. Its logs record each request: your IP address, the page address and your browser type. Sweep's own activity lines swap your IP and session for codes that can't be turned back into them. Render keeps logs for a limited time.
  • Modal runs the language model that reads your résumé text.
  • Oracle Cloud hosts the search server.
  • Apify runs Deep Sweep's searches, only if you connect your own key.
  • Google Fonts serves this site's typefaces, so Google sees your IP address when a page loads.
  • Resend delivers the feedback form to my inbox, with your email address so I can reply. Sweep keeps no copy.

Cookies

One signed session cookie, which ties your browser to your session for two hours after your last activity. Your browser also remembers whether you picked light or dark mode; that setting never leaves your device.

How the ranking works

  • Sweep reads your résumé for skills and experience, and you can turn each skill up or down before searching.
  • Quick Sweep searches company career pages and public job boards. Deep Sweep adds LinkedIn, Indeed and Naukri through your Apify account.
  • Each job gets a match score: how much of your résumé, skills and experience, the job actually asks for, weighted by your settings. A higher score means more of what you have.
  • Scores compare jobs for one résumé, not one résumé against another. A high score isn't a promise of an interview: it measures overlap, not what a hiring team will decide.
